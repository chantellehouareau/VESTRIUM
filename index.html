Privacy policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Who we are

This policy explains how Vestrium Global ("Vestrium", "we", "us") handles personal information collected through this website. It applies only to this site and to enquiries made through it.

What we collect

When you introduce yourself through our form, we collect the details you choose to share: your name, email address, sport, the stage of your career, your school or team, who introduced you, who is already on your team, and any message you write.

Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site secure and working.

We do not ask for, and ask you not to send, account numbers, contract terms, government identifiers or other sensitive financial information through this site.

How we use it

We use the information you send to reply to you, to arrange a conversation if you request one, and to keep a record of enquiries we receive. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who handles it on our behalf

We rely on a small number of service providers to run this site: our hosting provider stores form submissions and site logs, and an email delivery service forwards enquiries to our team. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads. These providers process information only to provide their services to us.

Cookies

This site does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Our hosting provider may set strictly necessary cookies to deliver the site securely.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of our correspondence, and then delete them. You can ask us to delete your information sooner.

Your choices

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us, or ask us to stop contacting you. To make a request, write to us through the introduction form on our Work with us page and tell us what you would like us to do. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, including the California Consumer Privacy Act; we will honor those rights as they apply.

Young people

This site is intended for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to contact us on your behalf.

Changes
